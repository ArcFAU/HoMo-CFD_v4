HoMo CFD
Version 1.0 - 2 October 2026

A local app for the aerodynamics CFD results of High Voltage Motorsports
(STAR-CCM+ on the NHR@FAU Fritz cluster): coefficient tables and forces,
section images, pressure distributions, 3D surface data with vortices, and
creating, starting and downloading simulations on Fritz. It replaces the
team's earlier CFD-Tool and runs offline on each member's own computer.

Using HoMo CFD
  Start: the HoMo CFD desktop shortcut (made once with "Create desktop
         shortcut.bat") or "Start HoMo CFD.bat". It runs without a window.
  Stop:  the power button at the bottom of the sidebar; it also stops by
         itself 30 minutes after its last window was closed.
  Your settings are stored per user in %APPDATA%\HoMo-CFD.
  More: README.md; STAR-CCM+ macros for the 3D export: starccm\README.md.
